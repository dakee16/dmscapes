Sunday Skillet Pizza

Prep 20 min · Bake 25 min at 450°F · Serves 4
Ingredients
  • 1 lb pizza dough, room temperature
  • 1 cup crushed tomatoes
  • 8 oz fresh mozzarella
  • Basil, olive oil, flaky salt
Method
  1. Heat the skillet on the stove for 5 minutes.
  2. Press in the dough, add sauce and cheese.
  3. Bake until the crust is deep golden, about 25 minutes.
